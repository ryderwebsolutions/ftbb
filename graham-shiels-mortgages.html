Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the questions first-time buyers ask most often. Don't see yours? Get in touch.

What is an Approval in Principle (AIP)?
An AIP is a lender's initial confirmation of roughly how much you could borrow, based on your income and finances. It's not a guarantee, but it lets you view and offer on properties with confidence.
How much can I borrow?
Lenders in Ireland generally use a multiple of your gross income, alongside a review of your outgoings and savings history. Every situation is different — get in touch and we can talk through your numbers.
Do I need a large deposit?
First-time buyers typically need a deposit of at least 10% of the purchase price, though schemes like Help to Buy and the First Home Scheme can help bridge the gap. We can talk through what applies to you.
Can you help if I'm self-employed?
Yes — self-employed applicants have different documentation requirements, and it's an area we can help you prepare for properly.
How long does the mortgage process take?
Timelines vary by lender and how quickly documentation comes together, but you'll be kept updated at every stage.
